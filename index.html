About Us

At HENTPRO, we combine expert linguistic precision with deep legal understanding to ensure that every document reflects integrity and intent across languages. Our work safeguards meaning, protects legal interests, and upholds credibility where accuracy is not optional — it's essential.

Our Services

Corporate

Precision that protects your business

We assist law firms, corporations, and institutions with translations of contracts, compliance documents, business reports, and international agreements, as well as interpreting services for meetings, negotiations, and court proceedings. Our work safeguards meaning, mitigates risks, and supports strategic decisions with accuracy and reliability.

Individual

Accuracy for life’s legal milestones.

HENTPRO supports individuals with certified translations of vital records, including birth certificates for visa applications, marriage documents abroad, academic records for study overseas, documents for foreign nationals marrying in Indonesia, and death certificates, wills, and testaments. Our translations safeguard the legal validity of your most important documents and ensure international recognition.
